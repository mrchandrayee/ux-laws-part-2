FIELD NOTE

People tend to accelerate their effort as they get closer to a goal. A head start can make the finish feel closer and increase follow-through.

RULENever start progress at zero.
Endowed progress effect · Nunes & Drèze (2006)

FIELD NOTE

Be liberal in what you accept, then normalize and validate it. Users should not have to guess the exact punctuation or formatting your system expects.

RULEAccept different formats. Clean them up.
Robustness principle · Postel's Law (RFC 761)

FIELD NOTE

Complexity never disappears; it is either handled by the user or absorbed by the system. Automate predictable work and keep the user focused on what only they can provide.

RULEThe system carries the complexity.
Conservation of complexity · Tesler's Law

LAW 05 — SERIAL POSITION

First and last stick.

FIELD NOTE

People tend to remember the first and last items in a sequence better than the middle. Place high-priority actions where they are easier to notice and recall.

RULEKey actions go first or last.
Serial position curve · Murdock (1962)
